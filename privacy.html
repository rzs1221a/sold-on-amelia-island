This website is operated on behalf of Kelly Marine and Will Henderson, licensed real estate agents with Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices Heymann Williams Realty ("we," "us"). This page explains what information we collect through soldonameliaisland.com and how we use it.

What we collect

We do not sell your personal information.

Calls and texts

If you check the consent box on our contact forms, you authorize Kelly Marine or Will Henderson to contact you by phone, text message, or email about your inquiry, including through automated means. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message and data rates may apply, and you may opt out at any time by replying STOP or telling us directly.

Service providers

We use a small number of vendors to run this site: Netlify (hosting and form handling), our email delivery provider (to route your inquiry to our client-management system), and BoldTrail/kvCORE (our brokerage CRM). Each receives only the information needed to do its job.

Cookies & analytics

This site does not currently run advertising or analytics cookies. If we enable Google Analytics in the future, this page will be updated to describe it.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to correct or delete the contact information you submitted, or to stop contacting you. Email us at Will@HeymannWilliamsRealty.com or KellyMarineRealtor@gmail.com, or call/text 904-321-6689.
